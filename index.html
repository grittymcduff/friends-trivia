🏆 The Shelf

How to play

  • ←↑↓→ or WASD to walk, or just click (or tap) where you want to go. Click a person or sign to walk over and talk to it. Hold Shift to hurry.
  • Space / Enter to talk, read signs, sit, and continue. Esc to back out.
  • In trivia, click an answer or press 1–4.
  • C opens your shelf of collectibles (wear hats, costumes and props from there!). L or 🏅 opens the leaderboard. M opens the map of New York (where you are, and who still has something for you). N toggles sound. + / − (or the 🔍 button) zoom in and out.
  • Keep your eyes open while you explore. Some very strange things turn up around the city at random…
  • A yellow ? over a cast member means they have a collectible for you. A white ? over a stranger means they have a quick question.
  • Walk into any subway entrance (from any side) to open the subway map.
  • Trivia difficulty:
  • Harder questions are worth more: ★★ for tough ones, ★★★ for Super Fan questions. Think you know it all? Find the Quiz Night host at Central Perk.
  • Your progress saves automatically in this browser.
